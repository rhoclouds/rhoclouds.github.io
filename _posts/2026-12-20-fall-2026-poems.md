  <section class="poem">
    <h2>1. <em>Untitled</em></h2>
    <p class="poem-meta">September 22 (Iambic pentameter)</p>
    <pre>
Persimmons leave when snow begins to fall
I never watched the snow come down with you
But we did see the leaves decay as one
    </pre>
  </section>

  <section class="poem">
    <h2>2. <em>Pinhole Projector</em></h2>
    <p class="poem-meta">September 23 (Iambic pentameter, ABAB rhyme)</p>
    <pre>
The trees caressed the stone for years on end
These days, the moonlight bathes the paths alone
As years pass by, the leaves will not descend
The crescents fade, if only they had known
    </pre>
  </section>

  <section class="poem">
    <h2>3. <em>Flight Delay</em></h2>
    <p class="poem-meta">September 24 (Trochaic tetrameter)</p>
    <pre>
Soldiers hurry onto runways
Lugging baggage onto chariots
Engines flutter into battle
Losing someone going nowhere
    </pre>
  </section>
