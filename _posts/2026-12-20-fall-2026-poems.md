<div class="post-subheader">
In fall 2025, I tried to write a poem a day for 21 days. Nearly all of them were free verse. This fall, I'm challenging myself to write a poem every day of the season, each following a defined meter. My goals are to see if the poetry is better overall and to see the rate at which my writing improves over the course of each challenge. I unfortunately missed some days and had to then write two on the following day.
</div>
  
  <section class="poem">
    <h2>1. <em>Untitled</em></h2>
    <p class="poem-meta">September 22 (Iambic pentameter)</p>
    <pre>
Persimmons leave when snow begins to fall
I never watched the snow come down with you
But we did see the leaves decay as one
    </pre>
  </section>

  <section class="poem">
    <h2>2. <em>Pinhole Projector</em></h2>
    <p class="poem-meta">September 23 (Iambic pentameter, ABAB rhyme)</p>
    <pre>
The trees caressed the stone for years on end
These days, the moonlight bathes the paths alone
As years pass by, the leaves will not descend
The crescents fade, if only they had known
    </pre>
  </section>

  <section class="poem">
    <h2>3. <em>Flight Delay</em></h2>
    <p class="poem-meta">September 24 (Trochaic tetrameter)</p>
    <pre>
Soldiers hurry onto runways
Lugging baggage onto chariots
Engines flutter into battle
Losing someone going nowhere
    </pre>
  </section>

  <section class="poem">
    <h2>4. <em>Untitled</em></h2>
    <p class="poem-meta">September 25 (Trochaic tetrameter, ABAB rhyme)</p>
    <pre>
Dormant pylon, retired steelyard
Amber silvers during sunrise
Metal enters, golden graveyard
Ocean currents cover white lies
    </pre>
  </section>

  <section class="poem">
    <h2>5. <em>Returning to No Flowers</em></h2>
    <p class="poem-meta">September 26 (Anapestic tetrameter)</p>
    <pre>
Since the limbs made the choice to let go, the leaves fell
In the frost, you were met with the blooms of late May
But my stems grew so bare and alone in the dusk
And now back with the Moon, I am told I must reap
    </pre>
  </section>

  <section class="poem">
    <h2>6. <em>Sirens</em></h2>
    <p class="poem-meta">September 27 (Anapestic tetrameter, ABAB rhyme)</p>
    <pre>
At the edge of the farm, in the hills of the church
With three steps, they can hear bells cry out in the night
A boy looks for the home, a lost man on some search
With six steps, the bell blares through the fog into sight
    </pre>
  </section>

 <section class="poem">
    <h2>7. <em>Worlds Apart</em></h2>
    <p class="poem-meta">September 28 (Dactylic tetrameter)</p>
    <pre>
Beautiful galaxy, silently shimmering
Distantly luminous, captured by outsiders
Picture us spiraling, tenderly orbiting
Forming the harmony, part of the universe
    </pre>
  </section>

  <section class="poem">
    <h2>8. <em>Wishing Star</em></h2>
    <p class="poem-meta">September 29 (Dactylic tetrameter, ABAB rhyme)</p>
    <pre>
Wandering over the evergreen, listening
Over the sycamore, edge of a memory
Past all the juniper, radiant glistening
Murmuring promises, left in a reverie
    </pre>
  </section>

  <section class="poem">
    <h2>9. <em>Masquerade</em></h2>
    <p class="poem-meta">October 1 (Amphibrachic tetrameter)</p>
    <pre>
Martinis envelop the ballroom in rhythm
Electric auroras refracting through glasses
The light of the candles emerging around us
Disguises awaken, concealing suspicion
    </pre>
  </section>

  <section class="poem">
    <h2>10. <em>Nocturne</em></h2>
    <p class="poem-meta">October 1 (Amphibrachic tetrameter, ABAB rhyme)</p>
    <pre>
The cashmere surrounds me, my senses departing
Dissolving in sea foam, forever receding
Adrift in the current, memories restarting
The lighthouse awakens, the feelings proceeding
    </pre>
  </section>

  <section class="poem">
    <h2>11. <em>Contando estrellas</em></h2>
    <p class="poem-meta">October 2 (Octosílabos)</p>
    <pre>
En medio de la noche,
cuando la luna brilla
a través de mis cortinas,
cuando mis ojos no cierran,
me pongo a contar ovejas.
Pero cuento las estrellas
que me recuerdan la distancia
que todavía hay entre los dos
Y la oveja se pregunta
¿Adónde se fue Polaris?
    </pre>
  </section>
